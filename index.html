About

This tool was crafted with passion by pablo-codes. I built this screen recorder cause all other screen recorders where paid . Most online screen recorders are heavily cluttered, require cloud uploads, or spam you with ads, or enforce arbitrary limits. PabloRec runs fully client-side on your own device—using the WebCodecs API and native browser MediaRecorders to encode WebM and MP4 files directly in memory. Built in mid-2024 for internal usage, it serves as a lightweight dashboard that respects user privacy and delivers high-performance utility without any bloat.
